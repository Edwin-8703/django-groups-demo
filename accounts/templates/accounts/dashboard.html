Multidisciplinary Collaboration

Combining the speed and agility of the private sector with the rigor and astuteness of academia, CEMA brings together experts from epidemiology, infectious diseases, mathematics, statistics, and data science to tackle complex health challenges.

Learn more →
📊

Data-Driven Decision Making

CEMA's data analytics initiatives such as the Kenya Health Facilities Survey and Census and the Kenya Health Facilities Assessment are some of the useful inputs used to drive impactful research and health policy decisions.

Learn more →
⚙️

Infectious Disease Focus

We employ epidemiological and modeling skills to understand the emergence, transmission, spread, and control of infectious diseases, mainly of zoonotic origin. Our research provides evidence that guides policies on prevention and control of these infectious diseases to improve public health.

Learn more →

Africa Data for Health

Combining the speed and agility of the private sector with the rigor and astuteness of academia, CEMA aims at comprehensively addressing these "Africa Data for Health" focus areas:

  1. Health Data infrastructure strengthening
  2. Building of Data Collection Digital Tools
  3. Data-driven decision-making and policy formulation
  1. Data wrangling and visualizations
  2. Applied epidemiological and mathematical modelling analysis
  3. Data literacy for healthcare professionals and policymakers
